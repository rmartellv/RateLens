The deficit is a curve input, not a footnote

US federal deficits run 5.8% of GDP in 2026, rising toward 6.7% by 2036 on current CBO projections. That trajectory shows up in the bond market as a rising term premium — now above 0.80% on the 10Y, the highest since 2011, and responsible for more than half of the recent move in long-end yields.

RateLens treats this as a transparent, adjustable decomposition: expected short-rate path plus term premium, with the deficit/debt trajectory as an explicit lever — not a black-box forecast.
